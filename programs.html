Our Football Curriculum & Core Pillars

Providing structured, age-appropriate coaching for over 65 boys and girls aged 5 to Under-20 in Arusha, Tanzania. Zero fees, all kits and training equipment provided free of charge.

Technical Mastery

Ball mastery, precision passing, first touch control, 1v1 dribbling, shooting accuracy, heading, and goalkeeping fundamentals.

Tactical Intelligence

Spatial awareness, defensive shape, transition play, pressing triggers, attacking movement off the ball, and game decision-making.

Physical Agility

Age-specific conditioning: coordination, balance, acceleration, core strength, reaction time, endurance, and injury prevention.

Character & Education

Discipline, academic monitoring, sportsmanship, teamwork, leadership qualities, and personal responsibility for future success.

Age-Appropriate Development Categories

Every child learns at their developmental stage. We separate players into dedicated age brackets to foster safety, confidence, and rapid growth.

Grassroots Stage

U6 - U8 Discovery

Ages 5 to 8 Years

Introducing children to football through joyful, high-energy games that build a lifelong love for sport and movement.

  • Basic body coordination, running, skipping & agility
  • Fundamental ball touches & fun dribbling exercises
  • Small-sided 3v3 and 4v4 mini-games without pressure
  • Fostering listening skills, cooperation, and friendship
Enroll Free in U8
Technique Stage

U9 - U11 Ball Mastery

Ages 9 to 11 Years

The golden age of motor skill acquisition. Players develop exceptional first touch, balance, and 1v1 confidence.

  • Two-footed ball mastery and turning techniques
  • Short passing, receiving on the back foot, shielding
  • 1v1 attacking and defending principles
  • Introduction to team positions and spacing
Enroll Free in U11
Development Stage

U12 - U13 Technique

Ages 12 to 13 Years

Bridging individual technique with team tactics, positional awareness, and competitive game understanding.

  • Long passing, crossing, heading, and shooting accuracy
  • Combination play: give-and-go, overlaps, triangular passing
  • Understanding zonal defending and spatial coverage
  • Academic progress check-ins and mentorship sessions
Enroll Free in U13
Tactics & Speed

U14 - U15 Tactics

Ages 14 to 15 Years

Transition to full 11v11 football, emphasizing physical strength, tactical discipline, and rapid transition play.

  • Team formations (4-3-3, 4-2-3-1) & tactical roles
  • Speed, agility, core strength, and aerobic endurance
  • Counter-pressing and defensive compactness
  • Decision making under defensive pressure and fatigue
Enroll Free in U15
Competition Stage

U16 - U17 Competition

Ages 16 to 17 Years

High-intensity match play, competitive youth tournaments in Arusha and Northern Zone, preparing for senior football.

  • Rigorous tournament match preparation and scouting exposure
  • Specialized positional training (striker, winger, CB, DM)
  • Video review, set-piece execution, and game management
  • Leadership responsibilities and peer mentorship
Enroll Free in U17
Elite Stage

U18 - U20 Elite Academy

Ages 18 to 20 Years

Elite preparation for professional club trials (NBC Premier League / Championship), college opportunities, and senior careers.

  • Peak physical conditioning and tactical sophistication
  • Agent, club scout, and showcase friendly matches
  • Career pathway guidance and contract readiness
  • Coaching badges & referee foundation workshops
Enroll Free in U20
Dedicated Programme

Girls' & Women's Football Development

Grassroots to Senior Women (Ages 6 to Under-20)

At Arusha Dream Football Centre, we believe football empowers girls with confidence, teamwork, and leadership. Our women's football initiative provides equal equipment, structured coaching, and female role models under Women's Football Lead Coach Neema Mwangi.

  • Dedicated safe training sessions and kit distribution
  • Confidence building, public speaking, and health education
  • Friendly matches and participation in Northern Zone women's tournaments
  • Pathway towards national team selection and collegiate sports opportunities

Our 6-Stage Player Pathway

From the very first touch in Sakina to national and international career progression:

1

DISCOVER

Ignite passion, enjoy the game, and fall in love with football.

2

LEARN

Build fundamental coordination, balance, and basic technique.

3

DEVELOP

Sharpen tactical awareness, physical fitness, and game sense.

4

COMPETE

Test skills in competitive matches and regional tournaments.

5

PERFORM

Demonstrate high-level consistency, resilience, and leadership.

6

PROGRESS

Advance to professional clubs, national teams, or education.
