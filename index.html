Arusha Dream FC: Structured Football Training for Boys & Girls (5 - U20)

Providing age-appropriate technical drills, game intelligence, physical conditioning, and personal character development to nurture future football stars.

Charity Mission

100% Free Access: Zero Fees for Training, Kits, or Equipment

Removing financial barriers so that every talented child from Sakina, Elerai, and greater Arusha can pursue their football dreams without financial hindrance.

Holistic Development

Education & Football: Developing the Whole Player for Tomorrow

We emphasize discipline, school commitment, teamwork, and leadership—ensuring young footballers grow into responsible citizens and successful professionals.
