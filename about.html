ABOUT ARUSHA DREAM FOOTBALL CENTRE

“Empowering Young Talents for a Great and Bright Future in Football”

Arusha Dream Football Centre is a youth football development centre dedicated to identifying, nurturing, developing, and empowering young football talents from an early age. Established as a charitable football development initiative in Arusha, Tanzania, the Centre provides free, structured, and high-quality football training to boys and girls from the age of 5 to Under-20.

We are a 100% Charity Football Centre where youth are trained completely free of charge—no joining fee, no playing kit fees, and no weekly or monthly contributions from children or parents/guardians. Training equipment including balls, training shoes, playing boots, and travelling tracksuits are provided for free through the support of sponsors and well-wishers.

Our goal is to create an environment where every young footballer has an opportunity to discover their potential, develop their talent, and work towards a great and bright future through football. We believe that football is more than a game—it is a powerful tool for talent development, education, discipline, teamwork, leadership, health, social development, and life opportunities.

  • Our Vision: To become a leading youth football development centre that produces talented, disciplined, educated, and responsible footballers capable of succeeding at local, national, continental, and international levels.
  • Our Mission: To identify, develop, and empower young football talents through quality football training, education, mentorship, discipline, and competitive opportunities.
  • Our Motto: “Dream • Develop • Believe • Achieve” — encouraging every young player to dream big, develop abilities, believe in their potential, and achieve their goals.
  • Our Core Values: Discipline, Respect, Teamwork, Hard Work, Integrity, Equality, and Continuous Excellence.
  • Holistic Pathway: DISCOVER → LEARN → DEVELOP → COMPETE → PERFORM → PROGRESS.
