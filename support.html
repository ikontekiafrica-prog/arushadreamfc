Invest in a Child's Football Dream

Arusha Dream Football Centre is a 100% Charitable Initiative. We charge ZERO fees to players or families. Every kit, football, boot, and tournament journey is powered by generous partners and friends like you.

❤ 100% Free Access for All Kids ⚽ Over 65 Active Players 📱 Mobile Money & Bank Direct 🤝 Transparent Community Impact

Why Your Support Changes Lives

In many communities across Arusha, talented children cannot pursue sports because commercial academies require expensive monthly fees, registration payments, and kit purchases. Economic barriers extinguish talent before it can even ignite.

Arusha Dream Football Centre eliminates this barrier completely. No child pays a joining fee. No child pays weekly dues. Every training session, boot, ball, and travelling tracksuit is provided free of charge.

When you partner with us, you are not just funding football—you are providing clean water, education mentorship, physical health, child safeguarding, discipline, and a beacon of hope for underprivileged youth.

“A child's financial background must never determine whether they get an opportunity to discover their potential and pursue their dream.”
— Engineer Matokeo Owden, Team Manager
Official kit distribution and player welfare handover in Arusha
