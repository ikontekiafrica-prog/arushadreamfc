100% Free Youth Football Registration

Empowering young talents across Arusha from age 5 to Under-20. No joining fee, no kit fees, no weekly dues. Every child deserves an opportunity to dream, develop, believe, and achieve.

100% Free Guarantee

Neither the player nor the parent will ever be asked to pay registration fees, coaching fees, or uniform payments. Our centre is a charitable mission backed by donors who believe in youth talent.

Education First Policy

Football must walk hand-in-hand with education. We expect all young footballers to stay enrolled in school, maintain positive academic effort, and show discipline at home and on the pitch.

Safe & Nurturing Care

All sessions are overseen by certified coaches and our dedicated Child Safeguarding Officer, Amina Bakari. We promote mutual respect, anti-bullying, and proper child welfare at all times.

Frequently Asked Questions by Parents

Is registration truly 100% free?

Yes! Absolutely zero money is paid to the Centre. No joining fee, no registration fee, no kit fees, and no monthly contributions. Our mission is entirely charitable.

What equipment must my child bring?

Children can simply arrive in comfortable sportswear. As donations allow, Arusha Dream FC provides official training jerseys, shorts, socks, and studded boots free of charge.

How are training schedules arranged around school?

All training takes place after school hours (late afternoons) and on Saturday mornings so that academic classes are never interrupted.

Can girls join the academy?

Yes! We have an active, dedicated Girls' and Women's Football Programme led by Coach Neema Mwangi with tailored, safe, and supportive training.
