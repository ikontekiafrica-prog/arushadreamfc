Safeguarding & Child Protection Policy

At Arusha Dream Football Centre, developing a footballer means protecting the whole person. The welfare, dignity, health, and emotional safety of every child remains our highest priority.

Safe Training Grounds

Regular pitch inspection, removing glass or hazardous debris, certified first-aid kits on site, and hydration breaks during all sessions in Arusha.

Zero Tolerance for Abuse

Strict prohibition of physical punishment, psychological bullying, hazing, sexual harassment, or verbal degradation across all age groups.

Safe Transportation

When travelling to away friendly matches or tournaments, players are transported in vetted, licensed vehicles with designated adult chaperones.

Equal Opportunity & Inclusion

Boys and girls of all economic and tribal backgrounds receive equal equipment, coaching quality, pitch time, and respect.

Academic Supervision

Coaches periodically review academic attendance and report cards, ensuring football participation encourages schooling rather than detracting from it.

Confidential Reporting

Any child, parent, or community member can report concerns in confidence without fear of reprisal or loss of squad selection.

Code of Conduct for Coaches & Staff

Every coach and volunteer representing Arusha Dream Football Centre signs and adheres to our mandatory Code of Conduct:

  • Positive Encouragement: Praise effort, sportsmanship, and teamwork; avoid harsh scolding or public humiliation.
  • Physical Boundaries: Never touch a player inappropriately; physical contact is strictly limited to instructional demonstrations or congratulatory high-fives in public view.
  • One-on-One Protocol: Coaches must never be alone with an individual child behind closed doors or private rooms. All discussions occur in open, visible environments.
  • Safety Gear & Hydration: Shin guards are mandatory for competitive games; clean drinking water is provided at every drill.
  • Parental Photo Consent: Media and match photos are taken with parental awareness to celebrate talent and attract charitable equipment support.
Reporting a Concern: If you observe any violation of child safety or coach conduct, contact our welfare officer immediately at info@arushadreamfc.com or call +255 752 553 876. All reports are treated with absolute discretion.
