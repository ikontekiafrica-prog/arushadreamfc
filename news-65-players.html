Arusha Dream FC Reaches Over 65 Enrolled Young Players in Sakina

Arusha Dream Football Centre celebrates a major community milestone, officially reaching over 65 registered youth athletes across boys and girls squads.

What began as an informal community effort to gather children from the streets of Sakina and Elerai into organized sporting activities has officially crossed an inspiring milestone: over 65 active young footballers are now enrolled at Arusha Dream Football Centre.

Ranging in age from 5 years old in the U6 grassroots discovery group to 19 years old in the U20 competitive squad, these players gather weekly at Sakina Ground and Elerai pitches for structured training drills, tactical sessions, and character education.

Our growth reflects the deep hunger among Arusha's children for constructive, healthy athletic opportunities,' stated Team Manager Engineer Matokeo Owden. 'Most of these children come from families where paying private academy fees is simply impossible. By making our Centre 100% free, we unlock doors that would otherwise remain firmly shut.'

With enrollment continuing to grow, the Centre is currently expanding partnerships with local well-wishers and international donors to ensure sufficient training balls, bibs, and boots are available for every registered boy and girl.
