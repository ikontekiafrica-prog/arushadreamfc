Sponsors & Strategic Partners

Join visionary companies, tourism leaders, and community organizations powering free football development, education mentorship, and character growth for over 65 young players in Arusha.

🤝 Corporate Social Responsibility 🎽 Match Kit & Jersey Branding ⚽ Equipment & Pitch Partnerships 📜 Official Partner Recognition

Building the Future of African Football Together

Arusha Dream Football Centre was established as a 100% charitable initiative. Because we charge no fees to children or parents, our ability to provide structured training, football boots, licensed coaching, and tournament exposure relies directly on strategic partners and corporate sponsors.

Whether you are an Arusha-based safari operator, financial institution, international sports foundation, or local enterprise, partnering with Arusha Dream FC aligns your brand with integrity, youth empowerment, healthy living, and genuine social impact.

“We seek partners who share our conviction: that investing in young people is the greatest investment anyone can make in the future.”
— Engineer Matokeo Owden, Team Manager & Director
Official partnership presentation and training gear handover in Sakina, Arusha

Full Academy Branding

  • Branded pitch-side banners during weekend home matches at Sakina Ground
  • Featured headline sponsor on our website, social media, and press announcements

Ensuring our 65+ children have safe training grounds and professional gear.

  • Funding of training pitch lease and ground maintenance in Sakina and Elerai
  • Provision of portable aluminium training goals and safety netting
  • Logo on all training bibs and equipment carrying bags

3. Tournament & Travel Partner

  • Trophy and medal presentation rights at club friendly tournaments
