Corporate CSR & Partnerships

Building the Future of African Football Together

Whether you are an Arusha-based safari operator, financial institution, international sports foundation, or local enterprise, partnering with Arusha Dream FC aligns your brand with integrity, youth empowerment, healthy living, and genuine social impact.

Corporate sponsors receive extensive on-pitch and digital recognition, front-of-jersey logo placement, and certificates of social contribution.

“We seek partners who share our conviction: that investing in young people is the greatest investment anyone can make in the future.”
— Engineer Matokeo Owden, Team Manager & Director
Official partnership presentation and training gear handover in Sakina, Arusha
