The Transformative Power of Free Youth Football Training in Tanzania

Many young people in Tanzania possess extraordinary natural talent but face steep financial barriers that prevent them from accessing quality coaching, proper boots, and competitive platforms. Arusha Dream Football Centre was established as a 100% charitable initiative where players are trained without contribution—no joining fees, no kit costs, and no monthly fees. By lifting this financial burden, we open the door for children from all backgrounds to dream, develop, believe, and achieve.

About the Centre

Arusha Dream Football Centre is a charitable youth football initiative in Arusha, Tanzania, empowering boys and girls from age 5 to Under-20 with free coaching, kits, and education pathways.
